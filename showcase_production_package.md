## Part 2: Complete ElevenLabs Narration Script

**Total Word Count:** 326 words
**Target Pace:** ~150-160 WPM (Words Per Minute)
**Voice Profile Recommendation:** "Adam" or "Rachel" (Professional, conversational, and technical tone).

```text
Welcome to the Politician Claim Consistency Tracker, an automated auditing tool designed to hold public figures accountable to their own record in real-time. Built using FastAPI, a Chrome Extension, and the Cognee temporal memory framework, our system detects factual and policy drift instantly.

Traditional fact-checking is slow, retrospective, and struggles with defining subjective truth. Existing automated checkers lack temporal awareness, comparing claims against future facts or missing the context of when statements were made. 

Our solution focuses strictly on objective self-consistency: evaluating a speaker solely against their own documented public record. 

During a live video, our Chrome Extension scrapes browser captions, groups words into sentences, and streams them via WebSockets to our FastAPI backend. The processing pipeline runs in four key stages. 

First, the Claim Extractor maps the incoming text to a structured Pydantic model containing the speaker, topic, and numerical or qualitative assertions. Second, the system queries Cognee using temporal search to retrieve the politician's latest prior statements on that topic, filtering out any claims that occurred after the speech date. 

Third, our Hybrid Comparison Engine evaluates the claim. If both the new and historical claims are numeric—such as tax or inflation rates—the engine deterministically calculates the absolute drift and percentage variance. If the claim is qualitative, it triggers an LLM-based Natural Language Inference classifier to evaluate contradiction or consistency. Fourth, the system adds the new claim to Cognee’s SQLite graph database for future tracking.

Any detected contradictions are immediately pushed back to the Chrome Side Panel in a high-visibility Neobrutalist warning card, presenting side-by-side evidence, dates, and original source links.

The tech stack is fully verified, leveraging Python, FastAPI, and Cognee’s local SQLite and Vector database schemas. A stability cache handles dual read/write tasks to ensure cost-free, rate-limit-proof demo executions.

In the future, we plan to support cross-politician analysis and auto-ingest official congressional transcripts. Join us in making political accountability immediate and objective.
```

## Part 3: Recording & Assembly Plan

Follow these steps to produce the final video:

### Step A: Generate Narration Audio
1. Copy the **ElevenLabs Narration Script** above.
2. Go to [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io).
3. Select a professional voice (e.g., **Adam** or **Antoni**).
4. Set stability to `65%` and clarity to `85%`.
5. Generate the audio and download it as `narration.mp3`.

### Step B: Record the Slides
1. Open [Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com).
2. For each scene below, copy the JSON block, save it locally as a file, and click **Menu -> Open** in Excalidraw to load it.
3. Switch Excalidraw to **Presentation Mode** (bottom-left play icon).
4. Record your screen stepping through the slides according to the timings in the **Video Timeline**. Save the recording as `slides.mp4`.

### Step C: Record the Live Demo
1. Run the backend: `make start`.
2. Open YouTube and stream a video of Governor Alexis Vance.
3. Open the Chrome Side Panel extension.
4. Record your browser window silently for **45 seconds** showing captions streaming in and high-visibility Neobrutalist warning cards popping up on the side panel. Save as `demo.mp4`.

### Step D: Assembly in CapCut
1. Import `narration.mp3`, `slides.mp4`, and `demo.mp4`.
2. Align `slides.mp4` transitions with the voiceovers in `narration.mp3`.
3. Append `demo.mp4` at `02:15`.
4. Export at `1080p, 30fps`.
